How the championship runs

Structured like
a real league

Every club plays a full home-and-away programme inside its tier. Promotion and relegation are decided on ice, not by invitation.

01

Three tiers, one ladder

Tier 1 through Tier 3, plus a Development stream for players in their first two seasons. Top two clubs go up, bottom club goes down.

02

Certified officials

Every championship fixture is worked by two registered officials and a scorekeeper filing to GameSheet in real time.

03

Safeguarding first

All coaches and team staff hold current DBS clearance. Under-18 registrations require a named guardian on the record.

04

Insured ice time

Registration covers league insurance, officiating and scorekeeping for the full programme — clubs pay for ice, not admin.
